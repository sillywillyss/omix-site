OMIX Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

1. About OMIX

OMIX is an internal analytics tool used to retrieve social media account and content data for analysis.

2. Information We Access

When an account is connected to OMIX, the application may access information made available through the relevant social media platform's APIs, including account profile information and publicly available content and associated metrics.

3. How We Use Information

Information accessed through social media APIs is used solely for internal analytics, research, and content analysis within OMIX. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Data Storage

Retrieved information may be stored locally for the purposes of analysis and historical record keeping. Access credentials and API tokens are stored separately from the OMIX website and are not published in the repository or website.

5. Data Sharing

OMIX does not sell or otherwise share retrieved social media information with third parties, except where necessary to operate the relevant social media API or where required by law.

6. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect API credentials and retrieved data from unauthorized access. Credentials are stored separately from publicly accessible website files.

7. Data Deletion

Data retrieved by OMIX can be deleted from its local storage by the operator of the system. Users may also revoke OMIX's access through the relevant social media platform.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the OMIX application or its data practices change.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the OMIX application operator.
